How the mechanism works

1) Blue gives us the real call, Second, call time, worker profile and AI summary when a call exists. 2) Job Board gives us the real vacancy, trade, location/rate and the First who posted it. 3) The engine compares trade + location + availability + conversation evidence. 4) If no Blue call exists yet, the candidate can still appear as an exact database match, but is marked NEEDS BLUE CALL rather than confirmed.

MATCHING LOGIC

What counts as a real match?

1. Worker identity

Worker ID links the worker profile to Blue activity when available.

2. Second

The Second is taken from Blue's real agent_name. If no call exists, the site says so explicitly.

3. First

The First is taken from Job Board's real created_by field for that vacancy.

4. Trade

Exact primary trade is preferred. Related trades are marked for verification.

5. Location

Worker postcode/area is compared with the job postcode or location.

6. Conversation

Availability, rate, documents and interest come from the real Blue call when one exists.
