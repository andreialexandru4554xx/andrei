REAL BLUE ↔ JOB BOARD MATCHES

Real recruitment situations only

Every match below requires a real Blue call and a real Job Board vacancy. No call = no match.

↔
Matching rule

1) A worker must have a real Blue call. 2) The call supplies the real Second, call time and conversation evidence. 3) Job Board supplies the real vacancy and First. 4) Only then do we compare trade, location, availability, rate and conversation. If there is no Blue call, the worker does not appear as a match.

VERIFIED BLUE CALLS ONLY

Blue calls matched to Job Board

MATCHING LOGIC

What counts as a match?

1. Blue call required

No real call means the candidate is excluded from the matching screen.

2. Second

The Second is taken from Blue's real agent_name.

3. First

The First is taken from Job Board's real created_by field.

4. Trade

Worker trade is compared with the live job trade.

5. Location

Worker postcode/area is compared with the job postcode/location.

6. Conversation

Interest, availability, rate and restrictions come from the actual Blue call.
