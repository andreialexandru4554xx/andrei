How the mechanism works

1) Blue gives us the real call, Second, call time, worker profile and AI summary. 2) Job Board gives us the real vacancy, trade, location/rate and the First who posted it. 3) The engine compares trade + location + availability + conversation evidence. 4) We classify the result as STRONG MATCH, FOLLOW-UP or BLOCKED. A worker is never treated as confirmed until the exact job is confirmed.

MATCHING LOGIC

What counts as a real match?

1. Worker identity

Worker ID from Blue links the call to the worker profile.

2. Second

The Second is taken from Blue's real agent_name, not guessed from transcript wording.

3. First

The First is taken from Job Board's real created_by field for that vacancy.

4. Trade

Exact primary trade is preferred. Related trades are marked for verification instead of auto-send.

5. Location

Worker postcode/area is compared with the job postcode or location.

6. Conversation

Availability, rate, documents and interest come from the real Blue call summary/transcript.
